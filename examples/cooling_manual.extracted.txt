# Cooling System
The cooling fan removes heat generated by the motor.
The controller monitors the motor temperature during operation.

## Operation
At high speed, the motor may generate more heat.
The fan runs while the motor is active.

### Thermal Protection
If the measured temperature exceeds the configured limit, the controller stops the motor.
An alarm is displayed until the condition is cleared.

## Maintenance
Inspect the fan and air filter according to the maintenance schedule.
Do not bypass the thermal protection system.
